How the 2x2 Pocket Cube Solver Works

The 2x2 Rubik's Cube (commonly known as the Pocket Cube or Mini Cube) consists solely of 8 corner cubies without fixed center or edge pieces. Because of this, one corner piece can be held permanently in place to serve as the orientation anchor, drastically reducing the search space to 3,674,160 possible permutations.

Ortega and EG Methods Explained

Speedcubers generally solve the 2x2 using the Ortega Method or the advanced EG (Erik-Gunnar) Method:

  • First Face: Build any solid color face (pieces do not need to be oriented in the first layer).
  • Orientation of the Last Layer (OLL): Orient the opposite yellow/white face using 7 key algorithms.
  • Permutation of Both Layers (PBL): Permute both layers simultaneously in a single algorithm.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is God's Number for the 2x2 Cube?

In the half-turn metric (HTM), God's Number for the 2x2 Pocket Cube is 11 moves. In the quarter-turn metric (QTM), it is 14 moves. Any scramble can theoretically be solved in 11 or fewer turns.

Why are there no center pieces on a 2x2?

The 2x2 cube is mechanically a 3x3 cube with the center and edge pieces hidden inside the core. The 8 visible blocks are exclusively corners with 3 colored facelets each.
