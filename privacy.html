Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At CubeMaster 3D (cubemaster-3d.online), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected and how it is utilized.

1. Camera Usage & On-Device Processing

CubeMaster 3D requires camera access solely for the optical color scanning feature to detect the 6 faces of your physical Rubik's Cube. All video streams and frame analysis are processed locally and entirely in your device's browser memory. No visual data, screenshots, or camera streams are ever recorded, saved, or transmitted to any external server.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

CubeMaster 3D uses standard local browser storage to retain user preferences (such as selected cube themes, shapes, and solver states). We also employ Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand aggregated user engagement patterns (such as page views and session duration) to improve overall stability.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors utilized on our platform. It uses cookies, commonly known as DART cookies, to serve relevant advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visits to this site and other websites across the Internet. Visitors may opt out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

We respect your privacy rights. You have the right to request information regarding any analytical data logged during your session or to opt out of cookie tracking directly via our cookie consent interface or by adjusting your browser settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require further clarification regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us directly at cubemaster3d.contact@gmail.com.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
