Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

At CubeMaster 3D, accessible from our official domain, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected and how it is utilized.

1. Camera Usage & On-Device Processing

CubeMaster 3D requires camera access solely for the optical color scanning feature to detect the 6 faces of your physical Rubik's Cube. All video streams and frame analysis are processed locally and entirely in your device's browser memory. No visual data, screenshots, or camera streams are ever recorded, saved, or transmitted to any external server.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other modern web application, CubeMaster 3D may use standard cookies and local browser storage to retain user preferences (such as selected cube themes, shapes, and solver states). These are used to enhance and optimize your user experience.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors utilized on our platform. It uses cookies, commonly known as DART cookies, to serve relevant advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visits to this site and other websites across the Internet. Visitors may opt out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

CubeMaster 3D's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or third-party web services. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers or networks for more detailed information, including their instructions on how to opt-out of certain tracking practices.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
